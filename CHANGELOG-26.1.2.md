# Changelog — Minecraft 26.1.2 (NeoForge)

Released versions are listed **newest first**.

## Port 1.1.2 — Minecraft 26.1.2

An unofficial NeoForge edition of Mielon's The Sift 1.1.2 for Minecraft 26.1.2.

- Adapted the **Sift dimension, 10 biomes, terrain, density functions, original structures, creatures, blocks, items and progression** to Minecraft 26.1.2.
- Updated **registries, networking, Ancient City portals, Rift travel, menus and GeckoLib animations** for the 26.1.2 APIs.
- Adapted **Willow growth, bonemeal, composting, Charoite enchanting and anvil restrictions** to NeoForge 26.1.2.
- Corrected **Ichor Regeneration I** contact and refresh behavior for flowing fluid, fluid-containing blocks and full Ichor cauldrons, without overriding stronger effects.
- Corrected **Dark Sniffer Sonic Sniff** so each wave affects a target once and preserves player motion across saving and rejoining.
- Restored **Fallen Civilization** and **Certified Sifter** advancement triggers for entering an Ancient City and visiting ten distinct Sift biomes.
- Adapted **Ichor rain and snow**, including Ichor-Snowy Peaks, to the Minecraft 26.1.2 weather renderer.
- Updated the animated **six-layer Sift sky** with smooth RGBA gradients and interpolated UVs for **Photon and Complementary Shaders**.
- Added **Photon** post-Iris sky compositing with forward-depth masking so sky effects remain behind terrain; no shaderpack ZIP modifications are required.
- Updated **portal, Rift, fluid, Sonorous beam and Singer effects** for the Minecraft 26.1.2 renderer.
- Added optional **Easy Magic 26.1.0** with Puzzles Lib support for Charoite enchanting and **LambDynamicLights 4.11.2** integration.
